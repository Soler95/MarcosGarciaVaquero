Biography

Marcos García Vaquero, born in Huelva (Spain), is Principal Trumpet of the Madrid Teatro Real orchestra, and Professor at the Aragon Higher Conservatory of Music in Zaragoza and Progreso Musical Madrid. He received his musical training at some of Europe’s most prestigious institutions, completing his higher studies at Musikene under the guidance of Luis González Martí, Reinhold Friedrich and Javier Simó. He later earned a Master’s degree from the Hochschule für Musik Hanns Eisler Berlin as a recipient of the Hezekiah Wardwell Scholarship from the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, studying with William Forman and Martin Wagemann.

His orchestral career includes membership in leading ensembles such as the Gustav Mahler Jugendorchester, the European Union Youth Orchestra and the Verbier Festival Orchestra. He has since collaborated with major symphony orchestras including the Staatskapelle Berlin, Deutsche Oper Berlin, Luxembourg Philharmonic, the Spanish National Orchestra and the Orquesta de Cadaqués. He has performed under the baton of distinguished conductors such as Gustavo Dudamel, Sir Simon Rattle, Daniel Barenboim, Semyon Bychkov, Daniel Harding and Juanjo Mena, appearing in renowned venues including the Berlin Philharmonie, Amsterdam Concertgebouw, Paris Philharmonie, Hamburg Elbphilharmonie, Vienna Musikverein and Teatro alla Scala in Milan.

Alongside his orchestral activity, Marcos García Vaquero maintains an active career as a soloist, chamber musician and pedagogue, and is a regular guest at international festivals and masterclasses such as the International Trumpet Guild (USA), as well as festivals throughout Europe and Latin America. He is a First Prize winner of the Alnafir Trumpet Competition and a Yamaha Artist.
