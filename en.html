About
Marcos García Vaquero

Born in Huelva and trained at Musikene and the Hochschule für Musik Hanns Eisler Berlin, Marcos García Vaquero has played under conductors such as Gustavo Dudamel, Sir Simon Rattle and Daniel Barenboim, in venues including the Berlin Philharmonie, the Amsterdam Concertgebouw and the Vienna Musikverein.

Alongside his principal trumpet position he is active as a soloist, chamber musician and teacher. He is a Yamaha Artist.
