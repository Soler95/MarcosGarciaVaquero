Biography

Career

Born in Huelva, he trained at Musikene with Luis González Martí, Reinhold Friedrich and Javier Simó, and at the Hochschule für Musik Hanns Eisler Berlin as a scholar of the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation.

He has played under Gustavo Dudamel, Sir Simon Rattle, Daniel Barenboim and Semyon Bychkov, and combines his orchestral position with an active career as a soloist, chamber musician and teacher.
